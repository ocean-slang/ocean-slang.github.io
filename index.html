Sarah E. Lang

Postdoctoral Researcher at Scripps Institution of Oceanography

I am a Postdoctoral Research Scholar in the Multiscale Ocean Dynamics (MOD) Group at Scripps Institution of Oceanography. I received my Ph.D. in Oceanography from the University of Rhode Island's Graduate School of Oceanography.

Dynamical and biological processes at the submesoscale are tightly coupled, evolving on small and fast spatiotemporal scales. My work focuses on disentangling the impacts of submesoscale dynamics on oceanic biogeochemistry by using a wide range of observational platforms, including satellites, aircraft, ships, and autonomous vehicles.
