2025 · Graduate Mentor · Wallops Island & Richmond, VA

NASA Student Airborne Research Program (SARP)

The SARP oceans group studied how phenomena such as wildfires, sea ice melt, warming oceans, and storms affect oceanic ecosystems and seascapes. Student projects spanned many different environments, from icy Prudhoe Bay in Alaska to the tropical Florida Keys. They leveraged a diverse suite of measurements, including satellite observations like PACE ocean color and Sentinel-1 radar, as well as field measurements important for the validation and interpretation of remote sensing. As a group, we conducted fieldwork at the nearby Virginia Coast Reserve, a dynamic coastal barrier island system. We flew drones to survey the coastal landscape and vegetation, which we could relate to quadrats we surveyed on the ground.
