Research cruises

October 1 – 17, 2026

Internal Wave Resolving Array NOPP2-Amazon Deployment Cruise

The National Oceanographic Partnership Program (NOPP) · Amazon River Plume

Wirewalker and biological sampling lead

  • Responsible for programming and calibrating instruments on the Wirewalker: Signature 1000 ADCP in high resolution mode (turbulence), RBR Tridente (chl-fl and backscatter), RBR Quadrante radiometer, SUNA nitrate sensor, RBR Concerto CTD, RBR ODO optical dissolved oxygen, and PAR sensor.
  • Tracked and forecasted the Wirewalker's trajectory using ocean forecast models and Lagrangian particle tracking, as the Wirewalker freely drifted with a surface float.
  • Led sampling for chlorophyll-a and nutrients for Wirewalker calibrations.
April 8 – May 3, 2023

NASA Sub-Mesoscale Ocean Dynamics Experiment (S-MODE) IOP-2 Research Cruise

North Pacific Ocean

Optical flow-through system lead

  • Responsible for the set-up, operation, and data collection of an AC-S Spectral Absorption and Attenuation Sensor (Sea-Bird Scientific), Imaging FlowCytobot (McLane Research Laboratories), chlorophyll fluorometer (Sea-Bird), and C-Star transmissometer (Sea-Bird).
  • Calibrated ship-based and autonomous-vehicle-based (e.g. Saildrones, Wave Gliders, Seagliders) optical instruments for the 3 S-MODE field campaigns. Created optical proxies for chlorophyll-a and particulate organic carbon.
  • Sampled and filtered seawater for biological parameters including chlorophyll-a, nutrients, pigments, and particulate organic carbon.
  • Contributed to the planning of bio-optical sampling strategies.
